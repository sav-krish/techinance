About us

Free STEM education, built for students

Techinance is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit making technology, neuroscience, and finance education clear, practical, and free for learners worldwide.

We offer interactive courses and Story Mode adventures so students can apply what they learn instead of just memorizing slides.
