Frequently Asked Questions

Do the courses cost anything?

Nothing. Every course, every Story Mode episode, and the certificate at the end are free. Techinance is a registered 501(c)(3) run by volunteers, so there is no fee and nothing to cancel.

Browse courses →
What courses can I take?

Four: Cybersecurity, Financial Literacy, Neuroscience, and How to Build a Start Up. Each one is split into units you work through in order.

Browse courses →
Who should take these courses?

Anyone, honestly. They're pitched at 12 to 16 year olds, but nobody's turned away.

Enroll in a course →
How long are the courses?

They're fully asynchronous, so you work through them whenever suits you. We'd suggest finishing within ninety (90) days though. Stretch it much longer and you'll start forgetting the early material before you get to the end.

Enroll in a course →
I have registered for a course. Now what?

Sit tight for 2 to 3 days while we send your classroom invite. Once that code lands in your inbox, you're in and you can start straight away.

Enroll in a course →
What is Story Mode?

Instead of reading slides, you play a role: a security analyst tracking a breach, a research assistant in a cognitive lab, a student founder building a company. You make the decisions and the story responds to them.

Correct calls earn XP. Wrong ones explain themselves before you move on, and finishing part of an episode earns a badge.

Open Story Mode →
Do I need an account?

You need one to save your progress. Sign up with an email address and a password, and your XP, badges, and checkpoint follow you to any device you sign in on.

Sign in →
Is my progress private?

Yes, by default. Nobody else can see what you have completed.

The leaderboard is opt-in and stays off unless you turn it on. If you do, you pick a nickname, and only that nickname and your totals appear. You can switch it back off at any time from your profile.

Profile settings →
Do I get a certificate?

Yes, on completing a course. It names the course and is issued by Techinance as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so you can list it on applications or a resume.

Can I use this on a phone?

Yes. The site and Story Mode both work on a phone, tablet, or laptop, and your progress syncs between them once you're signed in. Longer episodes are more comfortable on a bigger screen, but nothing is blocked on mobile.

Can I volunteer or teach with Techinance?

Yes, and it's how most of this got built. Volunteers write the lessons, tutor, and help run the courses, and most of them are students themselves.

Is Techinance a 501(c)(3)?

Yes. We're a registered 501(c)(3), and we sign off volunteer hours plus the PVSA award for volunteers. Heads up: PVSA is on hold right now.
