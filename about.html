About Techinance

We make essential learning free and accessible to everyone.

Who We Are

What We Do

Techinance is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Every course we publish is free, and every one is written and taught by volunteers who were students not long ago.

Our courses are built to be worked through rather than watched. In Story Mode you take a role, make the calls, and see what follows, so the ideas stick to something real instead of a slide.

Our Mission

What We Are Working Towards

  1. Access Without Barriers

    High-quality learning for every young person, at no cost.

  2. Skills for Real Life

    From online safety to smart investing, our courses equip students with practical knowledge for the world they live in.

  3. Reach Through Partnership

    We are growing our course library and actively seeking school, nonprofit, and community partners to bring free learning to more students.
