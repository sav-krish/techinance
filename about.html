About Techinance

We make essential learning in technology, neuroscience, and finance free, practical, and accessible to young people everywhere.

Who We Are

What We Do

Techinance is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit creating free virtual courses for young people around the world. Our student-led team turns complex, real-world topics into clear, engaging lessons learners can use with confidence.

We teach cybersecurity, financial literacy, neuroscience, and other emerging fields. Each course is designed to be accessible, practical, and relevant, regardless of a student's background or location.

Our Mission

What We Are Working Towards

  1. 1

    Access Without Barriers

    We are working to make high-quality learning available to every young person, at no cost.

  2. 2

    Skills for Real Life

    From online safety to smart investing, our courses equip students with practical knowledge for the world they live in.

  3. 3

    Reach Through Partnership

    We are growing our course library and actively seeking school, nonprofit, and community partners to bring free learning to more students.
