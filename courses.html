Interactive Courses

Experience role-playing as a newbie in the field as you learn more about your career, applying concepts into real-world scenarios. Earn a certificate upon completion!

Use the previous and next buttons, or the left and right arrow keys, to move between courses.

  • Technology · Ages 12 to 16

    Cybersecurity

    How technology actually gets attacked, and how it gets defended. You work through real breaches, take a phishing email apart, and learn to tell one kind of attack from another.

    All four units are also playable in Story Mode, where you take the role of an analyst in a security operations centre.

    Enroll Play Story Mode

  • Finance · Ages 10 to 16

    Financial Literacy

    How money really works: saving, credit, investing, and the traps that catch people out. Built around a first job, a first account, and a first very bad idea.

    Three of its four units are playable in Story Mode, where a credit union coach walks you through a first paycheque.

    Enroll Play Story Mode

  • Neuroscience · Ages 12 to 16

    Neuroscience

    What's going on inside your own head, and why your brain makes the choices it does. Design the experiment, read the scan, argue with the data.

    All four units are playable in Story Mode, where you work a placement as a research assistant in a cognitive lab.

    Enroll Play Story Mode

  • Entrepreneurship · Ages 12 to 16

    How to Build a Start Up

    Turning an idea into something real: finding the problem worth solving, pricing it, and surviving your first investor meeting.

    All six modules are playable in Story Mode, where you join a student founder programme and build a company across a term.
