Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Techinance (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to providing free STEM education to students around the world. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit our website, enroll in our courses, contact us, or otherwise interact with our programs.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information. When you use our contact form or email us, we collect your first name, last name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Enrollment information. When you register for a course through our enrollment forms, we may collect your name, email address, age or grade range, location, and other details needed to place you in a classroom and communicate about the course.
  • Volunteer or participation information. If you apply to join or volunteer with Techinance through our Work with Us form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, how you would like to help, and anything else you choose to tell us.
  • Story Mode account. Story Mode is the interactive part of our courses. To save progress, you create a Techinance account with an email address, password, and learner-chosen nickname. Supabase Auth processes the password. We do not store or display raw passwords in Techinance website code or progress records.
  • Story Mode progress. Your XP, badges, completed episodes, and place in an episode may be saved in your browser so that a lesson keeps working if your connection drops. It is also saved to our database under your signed-in account so it can follow you between devices. Your progress is private by default. You appear on the leaderboard only if you explicitly choose to share a display name and aggregate totals.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting or analytics providers may automatically collect limited technical data such as browser type, device type, IP address, pages visited, and approximate location. We do not use this data to sell advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to questions and support requests
  • Enroll students in courses and send classroom invitations
  • Provide course materials, certifications, and related program communications
  • Improve our website, courses, and educational offerings
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect the safety of our community
  • Communicate about volunteer opportunities, nonprofit updates, or donations when relevant

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for third-party marketing.

3. How We Share Information

We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. We use trusted tools to operate our programs, such as email, enrollment and classroom platforms, or website hosting. These providers process information on our behalf and only as needed to deliver their services. Messages sent through our contact and Work with Us forms are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, a third-party form service, which receives the name, email address, and message you submit in order to forward it to us. Story Mode accounts and progress are stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and handles sign-in on our behalf.
  • Other learners, on the leaderboard. The leaderboard is optional. If you choose to appear, other signed-in learners can see only your display name, total XP, badge count, and completed-episode count. Your email address, answers, scene history, and profile settings are never shown on the leaderboard. You can remain private or withdraw permission at any time.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Techinance, our students, or others.
  • With consent. We may share information in other ways if you ask us to or give us permission.

4. Children and Student Privacy

Our courses are open to a wide audience, and we recommend them primarily for students ages 12–16. We take student privacy seriously. Parents or guardians who have questions about a minor’s information may contact us at thetechinance@gmail.com.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for commercial purposes. If you believe we have collected such information inappropriately, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

A note for parents about the leaderboard. Story Mode accounts are private by default. A learner can appear only by deliberately opting in with a display name they choose. The leaderboard never shows email addresses, real names supplied at account creation, profile photos, answers, or learning history. You can also email us at thetechinance@gmail.com and we will delete the account and remove them from the leaderboard.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Techinance does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The site does use two kinds of browser storage, both of which stay on your own device:

  • Local storage. Holds a signed-in session token and a local copy of Story Mode progress. It never stores your raw password. You can clear browser storage at any time.
  • Session storage. Remembers that you have already seen the homepage introduction animation, so it does not replay as you move around the site. It is discarded when you close the tab.

Our hosting provider and any third-party services linked below may set their own cookies, which you can control through your browser settings.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including course administration, responding to inquiries, and meeting legal or nonprofit recordkeeping requirements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

Story Mode accounts and progress are kept while the account remains active. Account deletion is not currently available as a self-service page. You may request deletion by emailing us, and we may need to verify the request before acting on it. Deleting progress from your profile page clears lesson progress while keeping earned badges.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Story Mode data is protected at the database level: each learner's records can only be read or changed by that learner's own signed-in account. Nobody, including other learners, can read your answers or your history. The leaderboard is the one deliberate exception, and it exposes only the opt-in display name and totals described in section 3.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal or operational limits
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
  • Reset Story Mode lesson progress from your profile page. This clears your lesson state and XP from the signed-in account while keeping earned badges.
  • Request account and saved-progress deletion by emailing us. Account deletion is not currently available as a self-service page.

To make a request, email thetechinance@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

9. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website links to or relies on third-party sites and services, including Instagram, Supabase Auth and database services, enrollment and classroom platforms, and FormSubmit, which delivers form messages to our inbox. Those services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

10. International Visitors

Techinance serves students in many countries. If you access our website or programs from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other locations where we or our service providers operate.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website or programs after changes become effective means you acknowledge the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us at thetechinance@gmail.com or through our Contact page.
